How This Is Worked Out

Only orders that were paid are counted. Orders waiting for payment or that failed are left out.

We take 15% commission on every product, food and event sale. The seller keeps the other 85%.

An order counts as Events when its category is Events, as Food when the category name contains the word "food", and as Products otherwise.

Subscriptions are the real payments sellers have made. Featured listing fees are the charges on featured products, taken out of the seller's next transfer. Ad income is what you enter by hand.

Total earnings = commission + subscriptions + featured listing fees + ad income.

Seller Payouts

Send each seller their money. It shows up in their dashboard straight away.

How Transfer works: it updates the seller's portal and marks their paid orders as paid out. It does not send M-Pesa by itself, so pay the seller first, then press Transfer. Any featured-listing fees the seller owes are taken off automatically.
